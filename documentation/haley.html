Haley — fashion boutique

Editorial fashion store: charcoal and lime, Playfair Display headings, minimal product cards, mega menus in the header row, Deal of the week and bundles.

Theme file: haley-themePlayfair Display + LatoLime #DEFF35Minimal product cards

Overview

Install haley-theme-v1.1.0.zip (how). Everything below is already set in that file: you only need to create the content in the admin and upload your images. Shared steps (products, variants, swatches, metafields) are in Step 1 · Shopify admin.

Header in one row

Logo, menu with badges, search icon, account, wishlist and bag on a charcoal bar that floats over the hero.

Three mega menus

Shop (columns + product + banner), New In (products), Pages (image cards).

Minimal product cards

Reviews, square color swatches, title, price; quick view, wishlist and cart on hover.

Deal of the week

One product in detail with countdown, bundle, delivery info and payment icons.

Using a light accent of your own? Always set Text on accent color to a dark color and Accent text on light backgrounds to a deeper shade, as Haley does. See Accessibility.
