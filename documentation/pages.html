Step 3 · Pages

Templates shared by every theme in the package.

Collection & search

In the editor switch the page picker to Collections → Default collection. Three sections:

  • Collection banner: breadcrumb, description (from the collection in the admin) or fallback text, optional collection image.
  • Collection circles: round category shortcuts. Pick collections in Product grid → Category circles or add Category blocks. Collection images are used; square images work best.
  • Product grid: products per page, columns, card options, grid / list switcher, filter layout (horizontal, vertical or drawer) and sorting.
    • Sidebar: Categories menu (select Sidebar categories), Popular Products collection and the price slider range.
    • Filters come from the Search & Discovery app. Hidden filters lists filter names you don't want to show.

The search results page (Search in the page picker) has the same card and filter settings.

Grid and list views

Shoppers switch between 3 columns, 4 columns and a list on desktop. The list shows colours, reviews, title, price, a short description and an Add to cart button; quick view, compare and wishlist icons appear on hover. On phones the grid is always 2 columns.

Two-column header

In Product grid → Top of the product column, turn on Show banner above the products and Show collection title to place the banner and title next to the filters; then hide the Collection banner section.

The banner uses the collection's own image, or Image for collections without one. To show one banner on every collection (for example when your collection images are product photos), turn on Use this image on every collection.

All collections page

Open Collections list in the editor page picker. Each card shows the collection image, title, product count, short description (the collection description) and a Shop now button.

Product page

Switch the page picker to Products → Default product. Settings here apply to every product.

Shown automatically when your store uses them

  • Unit price (e.g. €2.50 / 100 ml): set Unit price on the variant in the admin.
  • Taxes / duties included and a link to the shipping policy, from your tax settings and policies.
  • Shop Pay Installments banner when the store offers installments.
  • Subscriptions (purchase options) when the product has selling plans from a subscription app.
  • Pickup availability when local pickup is enabled for a location (Settings → Shipping and delivery).
  • Gift card recipient form on gift card products: the buyer can send the card by email with a message and a date.

Other product sections

  • Product tabs: Description (uses the product description), Reviews (summary and sample reviews), Size & Fit (size table), Shipping & Return and Custom tabs. Add, remove or reorder them as blocks. Tabs style: Boxed (centred titles with + / −) or Underline (full-width row, active tab underlined). On phones the tabs turn into an accordion.
  • Product reviews: choose your reviews app as Provider (Shopify, Judge.me, Loox, Yotpo, Stamped), or Static to show the sample review blocks.
  • Related products (Shopify recommendations) and Recently viewed carousels.
Different layout for some products? Use the template menu in the editor to Create template (for example product.no-size), change it, then assign it to products in the admin (Theme template on the product page).

Bundle: buy more, save more

Product information → Bundle (buy more, save more): pick up to 6 products, the heading (e.g. “Buy 3 get 20% OFF”) and the discount preview. Shoppers tick items, choose size and colour, and add them all at once. The preview only shows the price: create the matching automatic discount in Shopify admin → Discounts (e.g. 20% off, minimum quantity 3). The same block is available in the Deal of the week section.

Product card & quick view

Global card options are in Theme settings → Top Market — Quick view:

  • Enable quick view and the button label.
  • Show add-to-cart / quick view bar on hover and Show compare icon.
  • What quick view shows: stock badge, rating, SKU, vendor, type, quantity, wishlist and “View full details” link.
  • Options to show (for example Size, Color), Option style (buttons or dropdown) and Color option style (buttons, swatches, or swatches with labels).

Per-section card options (image ratio, second image on hover, rating, badges, swatches, wishlist heart) are in each product section.

Cart & mini cart

  • Mini cart: with Theme settings → Cart → Cart type = Drawer, clicking the cart icon or adding a product opens the dropdown with View cart and Checkout buttons. It stays open while the page scrolls. Heading, note, shipping estimate, coupon and gift wrap are set in the same group.
  • Cart page (page picker → Cart): Items section with heading and free-shipping progress (threshold in whole units, e.g. 150 = $150; 0 hides it); Subtotal section with terms checkbox, order note, coupon field, shipping estimate and gift wrap product.
  • Free shipping bar: turn on and set the threshold in Theme settings → Trust strip. Enter it in cents (7500 = $75).

Wishlist

  1. Create a page with handle wishlist and template page.wishlist (Pages).
  2. In the editor open that page: set the default view (table or grid), labels, share button, empty state and benefits strip.

Shoppers add products with the heart on cards and on the product page. The list is saved in the shopper's browser, so it works without an account or app. Share wishlist creates a link that opens the same list on another device.

Compare

  1. Create a page with handle compare and template page.compare.
  2. In the editor choose which rows to show (rating, brand, type, SKU, description), rename the row labels and edit the empty state.

Shoppers add up to 8 products with the compare icon on cards, the “Add Compare” link on the product page, or quick view. The header compare icon shows the count.

Customer accounts

With classic customer accounts enabled, the theme styles Login, Create account, Account dashboard (orders, stats, default address), Account details, Addresses, Order detail, Reset password and Activate account. Open them in the editor from the page picker under Customer accounts to change headings and labels.

The Account details page is a normal page: create a page with template page.account-details and link to it from the account menu.

Blog, contact, FAQ, 404

  • Blog (page picker → Blogs): grid or collage layout, columns, sidebar position with search, tags and recent posts. In the List layout, List image shape → Landscape crops square or portrait article images to a wide frame. Card style → Boxed uses the homepage blog card (image, tag, title, date and comments in a bordered box). Blog post: featured image height, date, author, share buttons, previous / next and related posts.
  • Contact: contact items (address, phone, email, hours) as blocks, form heading and text, and a map from the Map address. Messages arrive at your store email.
  • FAQ: one Question block per question; Output FAQPage structured data helps Google show them in results.
  • About: Brand story sections with images and stats. Leave Accent color empty to follow the theme accent colour.
  • 404: heading, text, search box and a product collection.
  • Password page (while the store is locked): coming-soon text, image, newsletter form and colors.
