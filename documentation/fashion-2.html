Home Fashion #2 — minimal boutique

A light, minimal boutique: search, logo and cart on one row, a centred menu with banner mega menus, cards with small tag labels and a dark action bar, a Show more button, a collage blog and a contact page with info cards.

Theme file: occasion-fashion-2-themePoppins 14 pxPastels #C0DCDD / #F59DADCentered logo, no category button

Overview

Install occasion-fashion-2-theme-v2.0.0.zip (how). Every setting below is already in that file: create the content in the Shopify admin, upload your images and pick them in the theme editor. Shared steps (products, variants, swatches, metafields) are in Step 1 · Shopify admin.

Centered header

Search left, logo centre, wishlist and cart right; centred menu without a categories button.

Banner mega menus

Collection, Shop and New In open link columns with a promo banner.

Product cards

Small tag labels top left, one dark hover bar with quick view, cart and wishlist.

Inner pages

Collection with buttons under the cards, collage blog, contact info cards.
