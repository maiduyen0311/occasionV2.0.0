Navigation

Mega menus that show off your catalog

Product cards

Quick view, compare and wishlist on every card

Hover a product to add it to the cart, open quick view or compare it. Color dots swap the image, and badges call out new, hot and sale items.

  • Classic or minimal card layouts
  • Second image on hover, ratings and swatches
  • Quick view with options, gallery and stock status
Average order value

Bundles and deals that grow every order

Offer “Buy 3 get 20% OFF” right on the product page and in the Deal of the week section. Shoppers pick sizes and colors for every item and add the whole set in one click.

  • Gift wrap, coupon and free-shipping progress in the cart
Cart
